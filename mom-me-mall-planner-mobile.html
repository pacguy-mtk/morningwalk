How it works

Mobile readability first Big type, short cards, and large tap targets make it easy to read on the go.
Rotation memory Recently visited malls are still possible, but they are less likely to appear again.
Offline by default Everything is saved in localStorage. No backend, no login, and no internet needed after load.
